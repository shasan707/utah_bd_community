Our community gathers at South Fork Park in Provo on Saturday, October 3. Here is what the day looks like, and how to reserve your seats before registration closes.

BODY
Utah is a long way from home. Most of us came here for work, for study, or for the quiet that the mountains promise, and most of us learned quickly that the hardest part is not the winter. It is sitting down to a meal without the noise of people who know you.

On **Saturday, October 3, 2026**, we fix that for a day. The Bangladeshi Association of Utah invites every family, every student, and everyone who has ever been told they speak Bangla with an accent, to South Fork Park in Provo for our community picnic.

## What the day looks like

Come when you can, stay as long as you like. The park opens up to us from 10 in the morning.

There will be food, and plenty of it. There will be tea, and the kind of adda that starts with the weather and somehow reaches politics, cricket, and whose mother makes the better bhuna khichuri. The children will find each other within ten minutes, the way children do, and the rest of the afternoon will belong to them.

Bring your parents if they are visiting. Bring the friend who has been in Utah three weeks and does not know anybody yet. That is who this day is for.

## Where and when

- **Date:** Saturday, October 3, 2026
- **Time:** From 10:00 AM
- **Place:** South Fork Park, 4988 S Fork Rd, Provo, UT 84604

South Fork Park sits up Provo Canyon with open lawn, shade, and room for the children to run. There is parking on site. The drive from Salt Lake City takes roughly an hour, so give yourself time and come with a full tank.

## Reserving your seats

Registration is open now, and it closes before the event so that we can plan the food properly. Please do not leave it to the last week.

The form takes about two minutes. You tell us who is coming, you get a confirmation code, and you send the payment by Zelle using that code. Current prices for adults, students, and children are listed on the registration page.

[Register and pay here](/register)

If you have registered and want to check where your payment stands, the same page will show you.

## Raffle draw coupons

We will be running a raffle draw on the day. Coupons can be bought with your registration, either singly or as a bundle, and you will find both options on the registration form. Every coupon supports the running of the event.

## Lending a hand

An afternoon like this does not assemble itself. If you can help with setting up, serving, minding the games, or clearing away at the end, we would be glad of you. Write to us through the [contact page](/contact) and tell us what you would like to do.

## One more thing

If you are new to Utah, or you have been here for years and never come to one of these, consider this your personal invitation. Nobody at this picnic will ask you to explain yourself. Come and eat.

See you at South Fork Park.
